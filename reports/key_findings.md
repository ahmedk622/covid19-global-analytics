# Key Findings

Calculated from the analytical dataset: 197 countries, 2020-01-22 to 2023-03-09. All figures are cases and deaths as reported to JHU; see the limitations in the README before comparing countries.

- **Totals at 2023-03-09:** 676,568,028 reported cases and 6,881,787 reported deaths across the 197 countries (reported deaths / reported cases = 1.02%).
- **Most reported cases:** United States (103,802,702, 15.3%); India (44,690,738, 6.6%); France (39,866,718, 5.9%); Germany (38,249,060, 5.7%); Brazil (37,076,053, 5.5%).
- **Most reported deaths:** United States (1,123,836, 16.3%); Brazil (699,276, 10.2%); India (530,779, 7.7%); Russia (388,478, 5.6%); Mexico (333,188, 4.8%).
- **Highest reported cases per 100K** (countries with population of at least 1,000,000; 157 countries): Austria (66,188); Slovenia (64,057); South Korea (59,715); France (58,517); Denmark (58,070).
- **Highest reported deaths per 100K** (same population floor): Peru (666); Bulgaria (550); Hungary (505); Bosnia and Herzegovina (496); North Macedonia (464).
- **Largest single-day values:** 1,354,505 cases in United States on 2022-01-10; 59,961 deaths in China on 2023-01-15.
- **Largest peaks in the global 7-day average of daily cases** (peaks at least 120 days apart): 2021-04-28 (826,677/day), 2022-01-24 (3,436,886/day), 2022-07-24 (1,111,743/day).
- **Largest peaks in the global 7-day average of daily deaths** (same rule): 2021-01-26 (14,855/day), 2021-05-26 (11,771/day), 2023-01-21 (12,566/day).
- **Reported cases and deaths by calendar year:**
  - 2020: 83,777,901 cases (12.4% of total), 1,901,762 deaths (27.6% of total)
  - 2021: 204,948,804 cases (30.3% of total), 3,567,242 deaths (51.8% of total)
  - 2022: 371,734,966 cases (54.9% of total), 1,224,357 deaths (17.8% of total)
  - 2023 (to 2023-03-09): 16,106,357 cases (2.4% of total), 188,426 deaths (2.7% of total)
- **Recoveries:** the source recovery series ends 2021-08-04. On that date 189 countries had a recovery figure, totalling 130,898,355.
- **Reported revisions:** 171 country-days have negative daily cases (84 countries) and 199 have negative daily deaths. The largest downward revision of cases is 348,840 in France on 2021-05-20.

## Reading these results

- The largest single-day death value (China, 2023-01-15) is surrounded by days with at most 97 reported deaths (three days either side), which is consistent with a batch addition to the cumulative series rather than deaths on a single day.
- Per-100K rankings use a single population figure per country and depend on how much testing each country did.
- Rankings by reported cases are influenced by population size as well as testing and reporting capacity.
