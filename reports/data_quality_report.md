## Country standardisation

- 197 country names mapped to ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 codes using the JHU lookup table; regions come from the ISO country-codes table.
- 9 display names differ from the JHU name (US; Burma; Korea, South; Korea, North; Taiwan*; Congo (Brazzaville); Congo (Kinshasa); Holy See; West Bank and Gaza).
- Region assigned manually for: Kosovo, Antarctica (not in the ISO table's region fields).
- Province-level rows without a population match (cases kept, population not added): Diamond Princess (Canada); Grand Princess (Canada); Repatriated Travellers (Canada); Unknown (China).

## Recovery series

- Last date with a non-zero global recovery total: 2021-08-04. The source series resets to zero the next day, so later values are treated as not available.
- Countries that never reported recoveries: Antarctica; Kiribati; Korea, North; Nauru; Palau; Sweden; Tonga; Tuvalu.
